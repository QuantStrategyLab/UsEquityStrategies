# Phase 4: Frozen QQQM / TQQQ / BOXX development comparison

## Scope and identity

The initial `phase4_qqqm_tqqq_boxx_policy.v1.json` remains an immutable **HOLD** review draft: ex-date dividend receivables could enter budgets without historical known-at evidence. The `v2` policy separated retrospective economic NAV from decision equity, but its first runner had a member-reserve bypass, and the policy incorrectly rejected the supplied same-day processing/payment pattern; its six-path run is retained privately as **invalidated implementation evidence**, not a result. `phase4_qqqm_tqqq_boxx_policy.v3.json` is the reviewed and executed development budget input. The enhanced path has independent research candidate ID `qqqm_tqqq_guard_boxx_fixed_research_v3`; its matched-defense comparator has ID `qqqm_boxx_matched_defense_research_v3`. Neither is the original full `tqqq_growth_income` v2 candidate, the prior single-member BOXX policy, a personal allocation, or a paper/shadow/live configuration. The independent TQQQ/QQQ guard core contract and its builder remain unchanged. QQQ is signal data only. BOXX is a tradable ETF with its own price, dividend, liquidity and settlement risk, not Treasury cash or a risk-free rate.

The pair has one predeclared contrast: transfer five percentage points of target budget from QQQM to the existing TQQQ guard core while holding BOXX's 49% target and outer settled-cash 1% target constant. The 45/5 and 50/0 weights are deliberately small, round **research inputs**, not fitted to the seen returns, a recommended personal portfolio, or a search grid. The 5% TQQQ number is a *member budget ceiling*, not a promise of 5% TQQQ security value: the unchanged core may retain internal cash or activate its guard. The 49% BOXX and 1% cash amounts are intended targets; whole shares, fees, returns, settlement and receivables make realized fractions drift. No automatic capital curve is applied to this pair, so its member set `M` is empty for this comparison. The existing `capital_curve_policy/explicit_reference_v1` and its prior findings remain unchanged.

## Funding, risk and time contract

At each previous close, compute budgets from then-available **decision equity** and the unchanged guard-core decision from QQQ/TQQQ information available by that close. The retrospective economic ledger accrues earned dividends on ex-date, but a claim with no verified earlier announcement remains excluded from decision equity until a signal close *strictly after* its provider `process_date`; the date is a conservative research availability proxy, not proof of the actual historic announcement or provider delivery time. Without an intraday timestamp, the process date itself is not accepted for that day's close. At payable date, a dividend moves from economic receivable to economic cash, even when that date equals process date; it remains **restricted paid cash** until the later signal recognizes it and cannot finance a purchase before the following open. Missing process date, payable before ex-date or another invalid chronology stops the path. This separation avoids feeding a retrospectively accrued amount into an earlier budget while preserving economic wealth accounting; its conditional results must not be described as a historically verified point-in-time dividend record.

Apply effective-date splits to existing shares and ex-date dividend entitlements in the retrospective ledger before the following open; a future event is not inserted into an earlier decision. At the next common-session open, settle only proceeds and dividends already due, then sell excess whole shares, then buy TQQQ within its core target and member budget, QQQM within its target, and BOXX last. Previously sold shares do not finance same-open buys; the frozen research convention releases net sale proceeds at the opening of the second subsequent common session. Fees are charged once on each executed buy or sell. Member internal cash is reserved from **both QQQM and BOXX** purchasing power. The 1% outer cash target is protected on buys where funding permits, but is **not** a guaranteed post-loss or settlement floor.

The pair is long-only, with no margin or option overlay. The enhanced intended Nasdaq lookthrough target is at most `0.45 + 3 × 0.05 = 0.60` times prior-close NAV; QQQM and actual TQQQ market values, and TQQQ's approximate daily 3× notional, must also be reported separately each day. This target-level identity does not promise a 60% cap on realized after-open/after-close exposure. BOXX value is reported as a security exposure, not included in Nasdaq lookthrough; its non-equity risks are not set to zero. The unchanged TQQQ guard is an internal control and is not charged a second independent risk budget. There is no declared portfolio drawdown stop or implied risk preference beyond these research constraints.

The historical window is the 444-session common coverage from 2023-03-28 through 2024-12-31, after the pre-existing 61-bar warmup and 2023-03-27 first signal. The choice follows data availability, not observed returns. The entire span is seen **development**. Both paths use the same QQQ/TQQQ/QQQM/BOXX source manifest, same QQQ/TQQQ guard contract, same 5/10/15 bps per-side executed-notional cost scenarios, same whole-share and two-session settlement rules, and the same daily rebalance schedule. No extra price collection, cloud task or external state is authorized.

## Review and execution gate

The research objective is paired cost-adjusted net log terminal wealth and CAGR, evaluated together with maximum drawdown, maximum peak-to-trough dollar loss, peak-to-recovery time, fees, actual security value/NAV, cash/receivable/settlement and Nasdaq lookthrough exposure. The budget weights are fixed before the replay and never selected from its outcomes. The replay must recompute both accounts and the TQQQ builder daily; rescaling or adding returns to a prior NAV path is invalid. Missing source observations or failed ledger identity invalidate a path rather than changing the window or fabricating neutral states.

Astra gave GO for the exact v3 policy and, after finding the v2 member-reserve bypass, gave GO for its narrow implementation repair. The six v3 paths then completed with direct tests and private ledger checks. The v1 and v2 review/runner history remains available; the v2 numbers are invalidated even though the corrected v3 aggregates happened to coincide. These GO decisions do not confer verified historical announcement timing, strategy promotion, account access or execution authority. Any later material policy change requires a new policy version and narrow review; the frozen proportions and window are not edited in place.

## Validation

- Eleven relevant synthetic tests passed for policy identity, strict post-process signal recognition, same-day payment restriction, member cash protection against both QQQM and BOXX buys, unsettled sale exclusion, and drawdown/recovery measurement.
- All six v3 ledgers contain the same 444 dates. Each saved file matches its summary-bound SHA-256. On every row, three security values plus settled cash, pending sales and unpaid receivables equal economic NAV; decision equity equals prior economic NAV less unrecognized claims. Settled cash covers restricted paid cash and member reserve; lookthrough equals `(QQQM value + 3 × TQQQ value) / economic NAV`. Maximum daily accounting residual is below `4e-12` USD.
- A single terminal QQQM close perturbation changed terminal NAV while leaving all 443 earlier records and the terminal day's prior-close target unchanged. This verifies the affected prefix path; it is not a claim that every possible input or vendor timestamp is historically point-in-time.
- Astra reviewed v3 policy and the specific runner repair separately. Its GO is limited to the stated delayed-recognition development assumption and does not validate historical announcement timestamps or promotion eligibility.
